Methodology & sources

Returns use split- and dividend-adjusted closing prices at the last NYSE session of each month. Exact return ties receive average ranks. Average-rank ties go to the worst 12-month return, then 6-month, then 3-month, then ticker.

Data: Yahoo Finance’s unofficial chart endpoint. Missing observations block new signals. Source responses and prices are retained in Supabase. Alphabet uses GOOGL.

Based on Sheel Mohnot’s post. His screenshot describes rotation, but his sell-timing reply was undecided. Our monthly contributions and dividend treatment may differ from his version. His backtest has not been reproduced here.

Only confirmed fills count as strategy holdings. Net invested is purchases plus fees minus sale proceeds, not current market value. Performance is not yet calculated. This strategy concentrates in one company; no allocation cap has been set.
