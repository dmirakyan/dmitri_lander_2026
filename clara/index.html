05 / NYC FILING ROUTES
What filing actually takes.


PRIMARY ROUTE
NYC Small Claims
The court’s current instructions say there is no court-provided electronic filing. Prepare signed forms and payment for the clerk. Older text on the same page mentions outside services, but no working vendor is verified here.
Limit: $10,000. Fee: $20 per claim over $1,000; $15 at or below $1,000. Court staff initially mail notice to the defendant. Correct legal name and street address matter.
Form instructions allow one claimant and one defendant per form. Prepare separate forms, then ask the clerk how to handle related claims without overlapping recovery. The total is already below $10,000; splitting is not needed to evade the limit.

Legal points to preserve, without overstating them


  • GOL § 7-108 addresses reasonable deductions, a 14-day statement and refund requirement, landlord proof, and potential willful-violation damages. Confirm applicability and actual vacancy / refund dates. September 9 was within 14 days of August 31; a September 21 attachment alone does not establish when money was returned. Missing invoices alone do not automatically establish forfeiture.
  • Section 7-108(3) is an anti-waiver provision. It does not, by itself, decide Nick’s authority or the effect of an unseen settlement release. The safer factual statement is that Dmitri and Alex did not authorize it and disputed it promptly.
  • RPL § 235-b covers habitability. A full-apartment, seven-day rent figure assigned solely to Dmitri needs a documented basis; loss of one shower does not automatically justify that valuation. Record other bathroom access, actual room rent, construction conditions, notice, and inability to use the space.
  • The business’s own website lists this property and 41 E 20th St, New York, NY 10003. This is a business-address lead, not verification of the landlord entity or service address. Compare the signed lease with NY DOS records, and County Clerk assumed-name records if needed. Do not assume Clara has personal liability merely because she managed the tenancy.
